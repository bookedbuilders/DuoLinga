Act 1 · The instruments are dark

The instruments are dark

Before the first click was bought, the account had already lost the ability to learn from it.

Call trackingCritical

Call tracking is dead in all three metros.

A custom number-swap script formats numbers with a +1 that the native tags don't recognize. Debug testing proved only Dallas ever swapped.

Conversion goalsCritical

The only measured Sales goal is Page views.

81 results, valued at $81. The account is optimizing toward people who looked.

Primary actionsHigh

Junk actions sit as Primary conversions.

Directions, menu views, and engagement on account-default goals, teaching Smart Bidding the wrong lesson daily.

Closing the loopHigh

Enhanced Conversions tag: paused. Offline imports: never connected.

Three offline import actions were built and never wired up. The two mechanisms that would close the loop are both switched off.

FormsMedium

Form submissions are double-counted.

Once through the tag, once through GA4. What little is measured is measured twice.

GoHighLevelHigh

The CRM never talks back.

20+ cloned IVR workflows, Dallas and Florida forwarding rules with empty phone filters, and no conversion data ever sent back to Google.
